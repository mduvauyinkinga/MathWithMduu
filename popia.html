POPIA Compliance (How we protect your information)

Last updated: 2026-05-06

POPIA requires us to handle personal information properly. This section is a practical summary of how MathWithMDU complies.

1) Accountability

We take responsibility for how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

2) Processing limitation

We process personal information only for specific purposes related to providing learning content, managing subscriptions, and supporting users.

3) Purpose specification

We explain the purposes of processing in our Privacy Policy (privacy.html).

4) Further processing limitation

We only use information for purposes consistent with what you were informed about.

5) Information quality

We aim to keep user account information accurate and up to date.

6) Openness

We make key privacy information available via our Privacy Policy and this POPIA page.

7) Security safeguards

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore with security rules, and we apply reasonable controls to protect information.

8) Data subject participation

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (where applicable).
