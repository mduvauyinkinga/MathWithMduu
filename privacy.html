Privacy Policy (POPIA)

Last updated: 2026-05-06

This Privacy Policy explains how MathWithMDU processes personal information when you use our website. It is written for a South African audience and aligns with POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act).

1) What personal information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, grade, and your profile plan status (trial/paid).
  • Authentication data: provided by Firebase Authentication (you log in with email and password).
  • Payments: we collect your email address for Paystack payment handling and create a payment reference.
  • Content access: we store plan status and use your account to determine which lessons/past papers you can access.
  • Admin uploads (metadata): when admins upload files, we store file title, file URL, uploader email, size and upload time.
  • WhatsApp contact: if you contact us, messages and contact details may be processed to respond to you.

2) Why we process personal information (purposes)

  • To create and manage your user account.
  • To provide access to learning content based on your plan.
  • To process subscription payments via Paystack.
  • To allow admin staff to upload and manage content.
  • To communicate with you about your subscription and support requests.

3) Legal basis / consent (POPIA lawful processing)

Where consent is required (for example where you acknowledge processing in your account registration), we will ask you to agree during registration. For account access and service delivery, processing is also necessary to perform or conclude a contract with you.

4) Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies for essential website functions and, where enabled, analytics/tracking. When non-essential cookies/analytics are used, we provide a cookie consent banner.

You can manage your cookie preferences using the cookie banner and/or browser settings.

5) Who we share personal information with

  • Firebase / Google: for authentication and Firestore database storage.
  • Paystack: for payment processing.
  • WhatsApp / email providers: where you use those channels to contact us.

6) International data transfers

Firebase and Paystack are global services. Your information may be processed outside South Africa depending on their infrastructure and configurations.

7) Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. No system is 100% secure.

8) How long we keep personal information (retention)

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide the service, manage subscriptions, and meet legal requirements. When no longer needed, we take steps to remove or de-identify it.

9) Your rights

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (where applicable).
  • Complain to the relevant South African regulator.
